About Us

Built by Truckers, for Truckers

We've been in the trucking business for years — running our own fleet and dealing with the same problems every trucking company deals with: breakdowns, maintenance, body damage, inspections, and keeping trucks on the road.

For years, we handled those repairs in-house, with our own mechanics. Over time we built a shop around experienced techs who know trucks, know trailers, and understand what it takes to keep commercial equipment running every day. Eventually, we opened that shop to other trucking companies, fleets, and owner-operators.

Commercial Truck Restoration & Repair is an extension of what we've already been doing for years. We work on the same type of equipment we run ourselves, with the same mechanics and the same hands-on approach. We're not a shop that was built around trucks because they looked like a good business opportunity — we come from trucking, and now we offer that experience to you.

What We Offer

From mechanical repairs to full truck and trailer restoration, our shop covers the full range of commercial vehicle service:

  • Body & Paint — Complete truck restoration, collision repair, and professional paint & refinishing
  • Mechanical — Major and minor mechanical repair, diagnostics & electrical
  • Trailer & Reefer — Trailer repair, reefer repair, and custom welding & fabrication
  • Fleet & Compliance — Fleet maintenance and PM programs, DOT inspections

We also offer truck parking and handle lease returns, on top of our repair and restoration services.

Where We Work

Commercial Truck Restoration & Repair is located at 379 County Rd, Cliffwood, NJ 07721, serving trucking companies, fleets, and owner-operators throughout central New Jersey — including Monmouth County and the surrounding areas along the NJ Turnpike and Garden State Parkway corridors.

Whether you're running one truck or managing a fleet, we're here to figure out what's wrong, fix it properly, and get you back on the road.
