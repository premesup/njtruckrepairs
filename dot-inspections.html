FAQ

Common questions

Can you do the repair the same day if my truck fails inspection?
In most cases, yes — since inspections and repairs happen at the same shop, we can often turn it around in the same visit.
How often does a truck need a DOT inspection?
Annual DOT inspections are required for most commercial vehicles; we can also set up reminders as part of a fleet maintenance plan.
