FAQ

Common questions

How long does a full restoration take?
It depends on scope — a partial restoration focused on one area can take days, while a full frame-off rebuild takes longer. We give you an itemized estimate and timeline before work starts.
Can you restore an older or high-mileage truck?
Yes — restoration is often the right call for a truck with good bones but years of wear. We inspect it first and tell you honestly whether restoration or replacement makes more sense.
